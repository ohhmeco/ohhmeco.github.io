About

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Milan, working on quantum computing and artificial intelligence. My thesis, in collaboration with the University of Verona, studies quantum machine learning algorithms for facial expression recognition, with quantum memory models for data encoding.

Outside research I compete in karate and make music; I also design and build STLTH://SAMPLER, a music production app for Android, on my own.

Università degli Studi di Milano, in collaboration with the University of Verona
