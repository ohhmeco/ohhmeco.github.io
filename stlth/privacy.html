STLTH - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

STLTH is a music app (sampler, sequencer, DJ) made by Michele Bonomi. This page explains what the app does with your data.

Short version

STLTH does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Everything you make stays on your phone.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only to record sounds you choose to record (REC, HUM ONE). The audio is saved on your phone. It is never sent anywhere.

Music and audio files

The app asks to read the audio files of your phone only to open the samples and songs you choose, and to keep your projects in the folder Documents/STLTH. These files never leave your phone, unless you share them yourself (for example an exported WAV or video).

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions (PRO, SUB, plugins) are handled by Google Play. STLTH only receives from Google Play whether a product is owned. STLTH never sees your payment details.

Internet

The app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play about purchases, to check at the start whether a newer version of STLTH is required (it reads a small public file on this site; nothing about you is sent), and to open links you tap (for example the SUB CHANNEL on Telegram, or e-mail). There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

STLTH is not directed at children under 13.
